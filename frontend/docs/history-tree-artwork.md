# Ilustraciones del árbol de Historia

Generadas con la herramienta integrada de generación de imágenes a partir de la referencia visual aportada por el usuario. Son decoración; no representan un árbol histórico documentado ni sustituyen las fotografías de la comunidad.

Los cuatro PNG seleccionados se convirtieron a WebP con calidad 86, conservando su canal alfa y dimensiones. Los archivos publicados están en `public/images/history-tree/`; el manifiesto `src/data/historyTreeAssets.json` registra sus rutas y medidas. No se requiere generación de imágenes ni Python durante el despliegue.

Los textos y fotos siguen siendo HTML independiente y editable. El marco se refleja para alternar las ramas y se adapta a cada tarjeta. La copa, el tronco y las raíces se colocan con CSS; los degradados de máscara suavizan las uniones. Las ilustraciones son decorativas y están ocultas a los lectores de pantalla.

## Prompts seleccionados

### frame.webp

Create a TRANSPARENT PNG cutout sticker, landscape 3:2. A single horizontal oval frame made of thick naturally twisting living tree branches, richly realistic furrowed brown bark and lush forest-green and olive-green leaves clustered on its outside edges. The frame has one huge hollow transparent center occupying 75 percent of its width and 68 percent of its height. It is an isolated botanical object. True transparent alpha background outside AND inside the oval. Photorealistic nature illustration with soft warm daylight. Left edge has a thick trunk-like joining section; the other three edges are thinner curved leafy branches. Entire object visible with delicate leaf tips, no UI, no letters or numbers, no people, no other objects. Background: transparent.

### trunk.webp

The attached image is a STYLE REFERENCE ONLY for a botanical website. Create an original reusable illustration asset, NOT a website screenshot. Match its realistic, richly textured warm brown bark and lush olive / forest green broadleaf foliage, soft natural daylight, painterly photographic detail. No text, numbers, logo, UI, people, photographs, cards, icons, or watermark. Output a PNG cutout with genuinely transparent background, no white or beige background and no checkerboard painted in. This will be layered with separate live HTML and real community photos. Asset: ONLY a tall mature living tree TRUNK segment for the vertical spine of a web timeline. Very tall narrow portrait canvas, about 1:3 width:height. Trunk runs continuously from the center of the TOP EDGE to the center of the BOTTOM EDGE, no crown and no roots. Rich deeply furrowed realistic bark with restrained moss, natural sinuous gently winding silhouette, slightly thicker toward the bottom. Trunk stays in the middle 65% of the canvas, with transparent space on both sides. Several tiny green leaf shoots on the sides, no long side branches. The tree is upright, warm brown wood, very high texture detail, no stump cut face; top and bottom continue outside the canvas.

### crown.webp

La copa se amplió con la herramienta integrada de edición de imágenes para mostrar el contorno completo, usando la ilustración realista anterior como referencia. Se conservó el canal alfa al convertir a WebP. La composición CSS reserva espacio según su proporción 3:2, sin desplazar la imagen fuera de la página; en móvil, copa, tronco y raíces comparten un eje central.

Zoom out and extend the upper and side foliage so the ENTIRE rounded umbrella-shaped canopy is visible. Landscape 3:2 composition. This is an isolated realistic tree crown asset, not a whole tree, no roots.

### roots.webp

Create a TRANSPARENT PNG cutout sticker, wide landscape 2:1. Only the massive rooted base of a mature living tree, its thick furrowed warm brown trunk enters from the middle of the top edge and flares into broad gnarled buttress roots fanning almost across the full width of the image. Thick roots and intricate thin branching roots, small olive-green and forest-green leaves, moss and ferns between roots. Highly realistic bark and natural daylight, rich photographic botanical illustration. Isolated object with actual transparent background around and between the roots. No landscape, no background surface, no text, no UI, no white backdrop. Background: transparent.
